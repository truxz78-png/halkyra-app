Singapore cruise port guide

Singapore is the main cruise port of Southeast Asia. Ships sail from here to Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Vietnam.

Ports: Marina Bay Cruise Centre Singapore and Singapore Cruise Centre at HarbourFront

Nearest airport: Singapore Changi Airport (SIN), about 20 to 30 minutes by car

Weather: Hot and humid all year. Short, heavy rain is common.

Main routes: Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam

Before you arrive

Most visitors must fill in the SG Arrival Card online before they arrive in Singapore. It is free on the official website of the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA).

Check the visa rules for every country on your route, not only Singapore.

Getting to the port

Check which cruise centre your ship uses. Marina Bay and HarbourFront are in different parts of the city.

Taxis and ride apps are easy to find. The MRT train also goes to HarbourFront.

What to do in Singapore

  • Gardens by the Bay: giant tree structures and two large glass domes.
  • Marina Bay: the famous skyline and a free light show in the evening.
  • Chinatown and Little India: temples, markets and cheap food.
  • Sentosa Island: beaches and attractions, next to HarbourFront.

Useful tips

  • Try the hawker centres. They sell good local food at low prices.
  • Carry a small umbrella for sun and rain.
  • Singapore has strict rules. Eating and drinking are not allowed on the MRT.
