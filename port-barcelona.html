Barcelona cruise port guide

Barcelona is one of the most important cruise ports in Europe. Many Mediterranean cruises start or end here.

Port: Port of Barcelona, with several cruise terminals near the old city

Nearest airport: Barcelona El Prat Airport (BCN), about 20 to 30 minutes by car

Best season: May to October

Main routes: Western Mediterranean: France, Italy and the Balearic Islands

The night before your cruise

Arrive in Barcelona one day before your cruise. The city is a great place to spend a night or two.

Hotels near the old city and the seafront are a short ride from the cruise terminals.

Getting to the port

Taxis go directly to each cruise terminal. Check the name of your terminal in your cruise documents.

From some terminals, a shuttle bus takes passengers to the Columbus monument, at the bottom of La Rambla.

What to do in Barcelona

  • Sagrada Família: the famous church by Gaudí. Buy tickets in advance.
  • Park Güell: a colorful park with views of the city.
  • Gothic Quarter: narrow old streets, squares and the cathedral.
  • La Rambla: a long street for walking, close to the port.

Useful tips

  • Watch your bag and phone on La Rambla and in the metro. Pickpockets are common in busy places.
  • Lunch is usually late in Spain, often after 1:30 p.m.
  • A city pass can save money if you plan to visit many attractions.
