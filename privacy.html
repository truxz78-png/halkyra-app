Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Halkyra app and website handle information. Halkyra is published by DriftforgeDev.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account to use Halkyra.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address or payment details.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not show ads. We do not use the advertising ID of your phone.
  • We do not sell personal information.

2. Information we do not collect

Halkyra does not collect your location. Halkyra does not read your contacts, photos or files. Halkyra does not record what you tap inside the app.

3. Technical Services and Local Storage

Website hosting: our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it may record technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page and keep it secure.

Automatic translation: when your phone uses a language other than English, the texts of the page are sent to the Google translation service (translate.googleapis.com) to be translated. Only the texts of the page are sent. Google may receive technical data such as your IP address.

Local storage: the app saves on your phone the language you choose and the translated texts, so the page opens faster. It also keeps a copy of the page files, so the app can open without internet. This data stays on your phone. You can delete it by clearing the storage of the app.

4. Partner websites

When you tap a partner button, the website of that partner opens. Partners such as cruise lines, travel agencies, tour companies, insurance companies and eSIM providers have their own privacy policies. Their policies apply to the information you give them.

Some links are affiliate links. The partner may record that you arrived from Halkyra, so it can pay us a commission.

5. Children

Halkyra is made for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
