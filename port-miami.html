Miami cruise port guide

Miami is one of the busiest cruise ports in the world. Most ships from Miami sail to the Bahamas and the Caribbean.

Port: PortMiami, on Dodge Island, next to downtown Miami

Nearest airport: Miami International Airport (MIA), about 20 to 30 minutes by car

Best season: December to April. The hurricane season runs from June to November.

Main routes: Bahamas, Eastern Caribbean, Western Caribbean

The night before your cruise

Arrive in Miami one day before your cruise. Flights can be late, and the ship will not wait.

Downtown Miami and Brickell are close to the port. Many hotels there offer an easy ride to the terminal on the morning of the cruise.

Getting to the port

Taxis and ride apps go directly to each cruise terminal. Check your terminal letter in your cruise documents before you leave.

Traffic on the bridges to the port can be heavy on cruise days. Leave early.

What to do in Miami

  • South Beach: a long sandy beach and colorful Art Deco buildings.
  • Little Havana: Cuban coffee, music and food on Calle Ocho.
  • Wynwood: streets full of large wall paintings.
  • Bayside Marketplace: shops and boat tours, a short ride from the port.

Useful tips

  • Bring sunscreen. The sun is strong all year.
  • Many cruise terminals let you leave large bags with the porters at the entrance.
  • If your flight leaves late on the last day, you can store your bags in the city and enjoy a few more hours.
